FAQ

Questions from the crew

Windows showed a blue warning. Is it a virus?

No. That warning (SmartScreen) shows up for any app that hasn't paid for a code-signing certificate. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no paid tier. Voice and screen go directly between PCs, so it costs almost nothing to run.

How much internet do I need to stream my screen?

At 1080p 60fps, about 8 Mbps of upload per friend watching. Your screen is only sent to people who click Watch. On a weak connection, pick 720p when sharing.

A friend is stuck on “connecting…”

Some internet providers (with CGNAT) block direct connections between PCs. Try another network, like your phone's hotspot, to confirm.

Does it work on Mac, Linux or mobile?

For now, only on Windows 10 and 11.

How many people fit in a voice channel?

It works best with up to 6–8 people, since everyone connects directly to everyone else.
